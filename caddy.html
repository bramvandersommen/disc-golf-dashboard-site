Caddy

Shot-shape advisor

Pick the wind and the shot; the caddy queries your bag live for the disc that fits — and re-answers whenever the wind changes or the bag does. No disc is ever tied to a shape.

What it will do

  • Wind selector (calm · head · tail · L→R · R→L) that re-shapes every recommendation.
  • A shot-shape catalog — drives, approaches, utility, putts — each a query against the bag.
  • Wind-reactive flight path for the recommended disc, plus the angle/height cue.
  • Honest bag-gap flags when nothing in the bag fits a shape in a given wind.
Arriving once your disc behavioral data — effective stability and tags — is on the Discs tab. The recommendations read those directly, so the advisor is only as good as that data is real.
